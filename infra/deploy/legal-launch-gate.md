# Legal launch gate

The repository intentionally contains product-language placeholders, not legal advice or fabricated approval. Production is fail-closed until counsel and the accountable product owner provide final, jurisdiction-appropriate documents and record:

- `LEGAL_DOCUMENTS_APPROVED=true`;
- immutable Terms and Privacy version identifiers and effective dates;
- legal entity, governing law, dispute/complaints process, and contact email;
- adult-platform age assurance, uploader/performer consent and recordkeeping policy;
- creator/seller classification, earnings terms, tax reporting, DAC7/DAC8/CARF/VAT responsibilities;
- withdrawal/cancellation/refund handling for immediate digital access, events, memberships, live, messages, support, and later physical goods;
- content moderation, appeals, DSA/illegal-content contact and transparency duties where applicable;
- privacy retention/deletion/export, processor/subprocessor, international-transfer, cookie/analytics, and breach-response positions;
- sanctions, restricted jurisdictions, provider terms, payment/wallet disclosures, and noncustodial wording;
- accessibility statement and customer-support/complaints channels.

Approved versions must be rendered in the product, captured in acceptance evidence where required, retained in audit/payment records, and tested before staging approval. Do not set the approval variable merely to make a workflow green.
